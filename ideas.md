# Yash Solanki Portfolio — Reference-Matched Design Spec

## Ground Truth
The supplied reference, [dhlabs.ai](https://dhlabs.ai/), is the visual source of truth for this redesign. Match its calm editorial engineering-studio language: warm ivory canvas, charcoal ink, muted green emphasis, hairline rules, serif display typography, compact sans-serif body copy, and monospace technical metadata. The goal is a close structural and stylistic adaptation for Yash Solanki’s portfolio, not a copy of DehazeLabs’ content or branding.

## Portfolio Adaptation
Yash’s site will read as a personal engineering studio / field guide. The category line becomes “FULL STACK DEVELOPMENT · AUTOMATION · DATA SYSTEMS · BIOINFORMATICS”. The hero statement becomes “Software systems for complex data.” with selective muted-green italic emphasis. A bordered “currently building” readout replaces the reference’s deployment ledger. Numbered editorial sections organize capabilities, selected work, experience, education, and contact.

## Design Movement
Editorial Swiss-modernism meets technical field notes: spacious, high-contrast serif headlines, strict alignment, quiet rules, and evidence-led content rows.

## Core Principles
1. **Match the reference’s restraint:** no neon, no dark cyberpunk, no heavy glass, and no decorative gradients.
2. **Make typography do the work:** use serif scale, italic emphasis, and whitespace for hierarchy.
3. **Treat content as proof:** projects, skills, and experience appear as rows and readouts rather than card grids.
4. **Keep the system personal:** adapt the visual structure to Yash’s factual résumé rather than reproducing the reference company’s claims.

## Color Philosophy
Warm ivory creates the paper-like base and makes the portfolio feel editorial and considered. Charcoal provides strong reading contrast. Muted eucalyptus green marks emphasis, links, active states, and the rare moment of action. Pale beige rules define the system without boxing every element.

## Layout Paradigm
A sticky top navigation and a long-form editorial scroll. Every major section begins with a bracketed index marker and unfolds through wide rows: left metadata label, central explanation, right-side proof point or status. Large breathing spaces and thin rules create continuity.

## Signature Elements
- Bracketed section markers: `[ 01 ] WHAT I BUILD`.
- A bordered system ledger such as `CURRENTLY BUILDING / Y.26` with skills, projects, and status lines.
- Inline arrows, quiet underlines, and muted-green italic phrases.

## Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are direct and calm. Links gain a green underline, buttons shift slightly with an arrow, project rows open factual case-study overlays, and the navigation remains useful without turning into a control panel. Touch layouts remove hover dependency.

## Animation
Use restrained fades, slight vertical reveals, and short underline transitions. Avoid cinematic wipes, cursor effects, particle fields, and large parallax. Keep interactions quick and editorial. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System
Display: a high-contrast serif such as Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display for hero and section headlines. Body: Manrope or DM Sans for readable descriptions. Technical: IBM Plex Mono for labels, section indices, and system ledgers. Headlines are sentence-like and editorial, with italic green emphasis rather than filled accent blocks.

## Brand Essence
Yash Solanki is a Full Stack Developer who builds practical software systems where automation, data, dashboards, and bioinformatics meet. Personality: **precise, thoughtful, systems-minded**.

## Brand Voice
Headlines are compact and declarative. Explanations are specific and factual. CTAs are quiet and useful.

Use a compact `Yash Solanki` wordmark with a small square signal mark, visually aligned to the reference’s minimal studio wordmark. Keep the mark as a quiet signature rather than an oversized decorative device.

**Eucalyptus Signal — #2F6F5E**, reserved for italic emphasis, active links, CTA borders, and key status indicators.
