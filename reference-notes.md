# dhlabs.ai Reference Notes

## Ground-truth direction
The reference is a light, editorial, engineering-studio site rather than a dark portfolio. Its core language is off-white paper, dark charcoal ink, muted green italic emphasis, thin hairline rules, and small monospace metadata. It feels like a field guide or technical publication with a premium, restrained tone.

## Layout and rhythm
The page uses a sticky top navigation with a compact wordmark on the left, section links in the center/right, and a bordered CTA. The hero is a two-column editorial composition: a small uppercase category line, a very large serif statement with one italic green phrase, a concise paragraph, two outlined/filled actions, and a bordered “currently shipping” readout on the right. Horizontal ticker-like evidence lines and small deployment metadata bridge sections.

Sections are organized by numbered markers such as “[ 01 ] WHAT WE BUILD”. Major content appears as wide editorial rows with a left label column, a broad center explanation, and a right-side list of related modules or proof points. Hairline borders create large breathing spaces instead of cards. The visual hierarchy comes from typography, alignment, and whitespace, not gradients or heavy decoration.

## Typography
The reference pairs a high-contrast editorial serif display face with a plain sans body face and a small monospace technical layer. Giant headlines are sentence-like, with selective italic emphasis. Body copy is narrow and readable. Labels are compact, uppercase, letter-spaced, and often monospaced.

## Color and texture
The dominant background is warm ivory/off-white. Text is near-black charcoal. A muted dark green is used as an ownable accent for italic phrases, links, and selective highlights. Borders are light grey-beige. There is no neon glow or dark cyberpunk treatment. The visual texture comes from paper-like background, subtle rules, and typographic contrast.

## Interaction language
CTAs are quiet bordered buttons with arrow glyphs. Links underline or reveal on hover. Navigation is persistent and direct. The site uses long-form scroll rhythm and repeated evidence rows. Content feels active through labels like “currently shipping” and small status readouts, but the interface remains calm.

## Portfolio translation
For Yash, the correct adaptation is an editorial engineering studio portfolio: “Full Stack Development · Automation · Data Systems · Bioinformatics” as the category line; a serif hero statement such as “Software systems for complex data.”; a right-side “currently building” readout; numbered sections for capabilities, selected work, experience, and education; wide border-separated rows; and muted green emphasis. Existing résumé facts remain the only factual source. Project links that were not provided remain modal/case-study interactions rather than invented URLs.
